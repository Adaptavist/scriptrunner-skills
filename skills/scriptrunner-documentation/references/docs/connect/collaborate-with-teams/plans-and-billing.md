## Upgrade your Free Plan to Basic, Advanced, Pro, or Custom

Start the upgrade procedure from within ScriptRunner Connect. 

1.  Click **Team Settings** and select the **Plans** option.  
    The _Plans_ tab appears.
2.  Select your desired payment frequency, _monthly_ or _annually_. 
    
    **Card or invoice?** 💳 🧾
    
    Monthly payments must be made using a credit/debit card.
    
    Yearly billing allows you to pay via card or invoice and receive a discount. 😏
    
3.  Click the **Upgrade** option for your desired plan.  
    The plan summary dialog appears.
4.  Click the **Upgrade** button to confirm.  
    ![The ](/src/files/latest/194675438/339513197/1/1741363830000/click-upgrade.png)
    
5.  Complete the payment and billing details:   
        a. Select if you're paying via _invoice_ or _credit/debit card_, then click **Next**.   
        b. Elect to use the existing billing contact on file for the team or create a new one.   
        c. Click **Proceed to Payment**. If you opted to receive an invoice, it will be sent to your email. If you elected to pay with a credit/debit card, a checkout tab appears to complete the checkout process. 

A new plan confirmation appears. Your new plan is active and ready to use!

**Upgrade billing 🗓️**

Upgraded plans activate immediately. If applicable, your next bill will include the days of upgraded plan use within the current billing cycle.

Review the _Billing Details_ tab for a plan summary.

## Create a New Team on the Basic, Advanced, Pro, or Custom Plan

Follow the steps below to create a new Team and paid plan:

1.  Click the **Team Selector** under the _ScriptRunner Connect_ logo.   
    A drop-down menu opens.
2.  Click **New Team**.  
    ![The ](/src/files/latest/194675438/396297001/1/1751477588000/click-new-team.png)  
    1.  Give your team a **Name** and _Description_, if desired.
    2.  Click **Next**.
3.  Select your desired payment frequency, _monthly_ or _annually_. 
    
4.  Click **Select Plan** for the plan you want for the new team.
5.  Select your desired payment method: _Card_ or _Invoice_. Click **Next**.
6.  Invite the members you'd like to add to this new team by entering their email addresses in the provided _text box_, separating each with a comma.  
    Then, click **Next**.
7.  Please use your existing details if you've purchased a plan before, or provide us with a **Contact Name** and **Email** for _billing details_.
    1.  Click **Proceed To Payment**.
8.  Fill out your payment details and click **Subscribe** to activate your new team.

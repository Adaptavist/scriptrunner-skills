Find out about the features and how to navigate the ScriptRunner Homepage.

ScriptRunner Home provides a snapshot of your Confluence instance's capabilities, highlights current activity within your instance, and directs you to help resources and example scripts.

Choose Start scripting, Design your pages, or Run your automations to start working with some of ScriptRunner's scripts to help you manage your Confluence instance.

You can navigate to your [Execution History](../../features/logging/execution-history.md) and see your _Latest Errors_ on the next section of the home page.

Finally, you can quickly navigate to help resources, find [Example Scripts](https://docs.adaptavist.com/sr4cc/latest/scripting-resources/example-scripts), and watch a video walk through of Script Listeners right from your instance.

# Macros

- Platform: confluence-data-center
- Space: SR4C
- Hierarchy: Features
- Doc ID: doc-sr4c-79bed900-6403-47f3-b646-35650e476c86-e94031e4069ca624
- Source: https://docs.adaptavist.com/sr4c/latest/features#macros--en

Macros can be configured to enhance the content on your Confluence pages.

Macros make your organization's pages easier to use for both editors and viewers by providing them with the information they need or smarter ways to build content.

Macros are divided into two types:

-   [Create Custom Macro](https://docs.adaptavist.com/sr4c/latest/features/macros/create-custom-macro)
-   [Built-In Macros](https://docs.adaptavist.com/sr4c/latest/features/macros/built-in-macros)

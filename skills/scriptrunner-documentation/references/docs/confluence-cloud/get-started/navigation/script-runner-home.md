# ScriptRunner Home

- Platform: confluence-cloud
- Space: SR4CC
- Hierarchy: get-started > navigation
- Doc ID: doc-sr4cc-445219737
- Source: https://docs.adaptavist.com/sr4cc/latest/get-started/navigation/scriptrunner-home

ScriptRunner Home provides a snapshot of your Confluence instance's capabilities, highlights current activity within your instance, and directs you to help resources and example scripts. 

Choose **Start scripting**, **Design your pages**, or **Run your automations** to start working with some of ScriptRunner's scripts to help you manage your Confluence instance.

![](/sr4cc/files/latest/445219737/477863929/1/1765568066000/homepage-1.png)

You can navigate to your [Execution History](https://docs.adaptavist.com/sr4cc/latest/features/logging/execution-history) and see your _Latest Errors_ on the next section of the home page.

![](/sr4cc/files/latest/445219737/445219740/1/1759332522000/home-2.png)

Finally, you can quickly navigate to help resources, find [Example Scripts](https://docs.adaptavist.com/sr4cc/latest/scripting-resources/example-scripts), and watch a video walk through of Script Listeners right from your instance. 

![](/sr4cc/files/latest/445219737/445219739/1/1759332609000/home-3.png)

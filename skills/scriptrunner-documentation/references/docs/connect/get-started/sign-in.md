Learn how to create a ScriptRunner Connect account and sign in to the app.

To sign in to and use ScriptRunner Connect, you must have a ScriptRunner Connect account. Create a new ScriptRunner Connect account or sign in using your existing Google credentials.

Choose one of the following options and follow the steps.

## Register a new account using email/password 📝

1.  Click the Sign Up tab.
2.  Enter your _email address_ and _password_.
3.  Enter your _first_ and _last name_.
4.  Click Sign Up.
    
    The _Consent Required_ modal appears.
    
5.  Check the I agree to the EULA and terms/policies box, then click I AGREE.
    
    ScriptRunner Connect opens, and you are signed in.
    

## Register a new account using Google credentials 👤

1.  Click the Sign Up tab.
2.  Click Sign Up with Google.
3.  Select a _Google profile_ you want to connect to ScriptRunner Connect.
    
    The _Consent Required_ modal appears.
    
4.  Check the I agree to the Terms and EULA box.
    
    ScriptRunner Connect opens, and you are signed in.
    

## Sign in with Google credentials 🚪

Follow this process to log in to ScriptRunner Connect once you've registered an app account.

1.  On the Log In tab, click Sign in with Google.
2.  Select a _Google profile_ you want to connect to ScriptRunner Connect.
    
    ScriptRunner Connect opens, and you are signed in.

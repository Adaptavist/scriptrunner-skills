## New ways to share your ideas, experiences and opinions

Discover how to share your ideas, experiences, and feedback with the ScriptRunner team through our dedicated Get Involved area.

We have created a dedicated area on our website which is your exclusive gateway to the ScriptRunner teams and having your voice heard! From here, you can:

-   Request a feature idea
-   Contribute to [Example scripts](https://www.scriptrunnerhq.com/help/example-scripts?ScriptRunner%5BrefinementList%5D%5Bapp%5D%5B0%5D=script-runner-jira&ScriptRunner%5BrefinementList%5D%5Bplatform%5D%5B0%5D=cloud) on the [ScriptRunner website](https://www.scriptrunnerhq.com/)

-   Sign up for paid research opportunities
-   Get facetime with our product managers and dev team

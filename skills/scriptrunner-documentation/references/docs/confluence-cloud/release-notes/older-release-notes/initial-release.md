Part of the [ScriptRunner family](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/search?query=scriptrunner%2520adaptavist), ScriptRunner for Confluence gives you the power and flexibility to customize, extend, and automate Confluence. It offers a wide range of code-free, built-in features, and it gives you the ability to create your own customizations. 

Some of the benefits of ScriptRunner include:

-   Add your own scripts to Confluence
    
-   Reduce your admin workload without writing a full app by executing custom actions in Confluence using Groovy
-   Maintain your Confluence instance easily and efficiently with eight built-in scripts
-   Automate regular tasks
    
-   Schedule tasks to run at specific times, which automates functionality and takes advantage of low demand periods
    
-   Integrate with Jira and other systems and apps to automate functionality, promote good practice, and ensure tasks/content aren't lost

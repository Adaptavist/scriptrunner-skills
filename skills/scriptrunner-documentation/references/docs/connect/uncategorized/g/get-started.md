Welcome, everyone, to the app! How about a brief introduction?

Our app, [ScriptRunner Connect](https://app.scriptrunnerconnect.com/), provides **Atlassian and system administrators,** as well as Atlassian consultants, with an **assisted coding** **environment** to script all their **Atlassian-to-Atlassian and Atlassian-to-third-party integrations**, all in one consolidated hub. ScriptRunner Connect lets you build more complete integrations than limited point-and-click tools **without advanced development skills**, so you can solve complex integration challenges.

ScriptRunner Connect is built by Adaptavist, an **Atlassian leader and the maker of ScriptRunner**, but it's one of the few **Atlassian-to-many cloud-based integration apps** in the market that focuses on the Atlassian toolset. That makes it the only Atlassian integration tool you'll ever need to consolidate your connections, reducing costs, learning, and implementation time while fully solving your advanced integration challenges.

## Why we made the app

Neither enterprise and bespoke solutions nor single-purpose integration tools meet the current needs of organizations trying to automate business processes around Atlassian and third-party apps, and none of these options are simple or efficient to manage.

Using any of these alternatives often results in **slow implementation times** and **a high number of resource and budget requests that may not be available to system administrator**s or Atlassian consultants.

A lack of consolidation could also lead to the proliferation of integration tools and an increase in the complexity of your system. Plus, **system admins need to** **automate processes across their tools ecosystem** and have little to no time to learn and understand each of the tools required to connect all these apps.

Furthermore, most existing alternatives on the market aren't budget-friendly or easy to implement, and often require IT or development resources to integrate them into their systems.

Not good. Quite bad, actually. So we decided to build [something better](https://app.scriptrunnerconnect.com/).
